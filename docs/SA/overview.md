## 🎯 Responsibilities

- Define end-to-end technical solutions aligned with business requirements
- Evaluate and select appropriate technologies, frameworks, and platforms
- Create architecture diagrams, technical specifications, and design documents
- Ensure non-functional requirements (scalability, security, performance, reliability) are met
- Guide development teams on implementation approach and best practices
- Conduct architecture reviews and provide governance across projects
- Manage technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Bridge communication between business stakeholders and engineering teams
